## Today's Project

### Chemical Plant KPI Analysis

I created a small synthetic chemical plant dataset containing:

* Feed quantity
* Product quantity
* Energy consumption
* Downtime
* Yield
* Energy intensity

The dataset is created purely for learning and does not contain confidential company information.

## What I Learned Today

* Creating and structuring an Excel Table
* Using structured references
* Calculating product yield
* Calculating energy intensity
* Using SUM and AVERAGE
* Creating a KPI summary
* Creating and interpreting a PivotTable
* Changing PivotTable calculations from Sum to Average
* Creating simple KPI charts
* Comparing plant units using normalized performance indicators

## Unit Performance

| KPI                      |    Unit A |    Unit B |
| ------------------------ | --------: | --------: |
| Feed processed           |   1,239 t |   1,410 t |
| Product produced         |   1,118 t |   1,290 t |
| Total energy             |  3,597 GJ |  4,087 GJ |
| Average yield            |    90.22% |    91.48% |
| Average energy intensity | 3.22 GJ/t | 3.17 GJ/t |

## Key Observation

Unit B processed more feed and produced more product than Unit A.

More importantly, Unit B also showed a slightly higher average yield and lower average energy intensity.

Although Unit B consumed more total energy, it also processed and produced more material. Therefore, total energy consumption alone is not enough to compare energy efficiency between the two units.

Energy intensity provides a more useful normalized comparison.

## Questions for Further Analysis

* Why does Unit B have a higher average yield?
* What factors may contribute to the difference in energy intensity?
* Does downtime affect yield?
* Does production rate affect energy intensity?
* Are there specific days with unusually poor performance?
* Would the conclusions remain the same with a larger dataset?
